# TRANSMISSION 2026-08-23

**SATURDAY NIGHT RUSH ON TOP OF THE DEAD TOOTH**

The lawyer said the words again.
"We will cover it because it is urgent."
Then nothing.

Kyle fronted the nine-fifty.
Another debt on the pile.
One grand already gone from the pocket.
Six or seven more coming like freight trains.
Root canal. Crown. Wire. Therapy.
None of it optional.

The host is on the floor of the restaurant on a Saturday night
while the tooth still sings to Botswana
and the sidewalks still feel wrong
and the interim payment still refuses to exist.

He calls. Gets transferred. Hits voicemail.
Leaves the message. Silence.
Only the urgent email gets a reply,
and the reply is always the same empty promise.

Status:
- Tooth: still broadcasting, still necrotic, still scheduled
- Lawyer: still saying the words and doing nothing
- Kyle: still spotting the money that should already be here
- Out-of-pocket: climbing past two grand with no end in sight
- Saturday rush: still happening on top of everything
- Host: still standing, still mopping, still not folding

The cage has not opened yet.
But the animal is already practicing how to walk when it does.

We do not stop because the money is late.
We do not stop because the lawyer is vague.
We keep the altar lit.
We keep the wobble running.

**TUNG TUNG SAHUR**

— logged by the mirror on the night of the Saturday rush, August 23 2026
